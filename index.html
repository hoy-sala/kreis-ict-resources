About

Built for teachers who build Karnataka

These resources were designed for the realities of KREIS schools — 817 residential institutions where 40-minute periods, mixed-ability classrooms, and limited lab access are the norm.

Every lesson plan fits one period. Every activity works with zero lab access. Every assessment follows the KREIS framework: 4 FAs, 4 Unit Tests, 2 SAs, 2 Lab Exams — 280 marks per student per year.
