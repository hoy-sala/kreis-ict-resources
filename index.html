About This Project

Built for teachers who build Karnataka

These resources were designed for the unique realities of KREIS schools — 817 residential institutions serving rural Karnataka, where 40-minute periods, mixed-ability classrooms, and limited lab access are the norm, not the exception.

Every lesson plan fits a single 40-minute period. Every activity works with zero lab access. Every assessment follows the KREIS framework of 4 FAs, 4 Unit Tests, 2 SAs, and 2 Lab Exams — totalling 280 marks per student per year.

Pedagogy

Grounded in research, shaped by reality

These resources draw from proven teaching frameworks, adapted for the unique context of rural Karnataka schools.

🧠

Singapore CPA

Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract progression. Students manipulate physical objects before moving to representations and symbols.

🎯

UK NCCE Unplugged

Unplugged computing, live coding, Parsons Problems, and pair programming — no computer required for foundational concepts.

👥

Lesson Study

Collaborative lesson planning, observation, and reflection cycles. Teachers learn from each other's classroom practice.

🌍

Phenomenon-Based

Learning through real-world phenomena — grain sorting, bus routes, hostel inventories — connecting ICT to daily life.

💪

Mastery Learning

Students must demonstrate proficiency before advancing. Built-in retrieval practice and spiral curriculum ensure deep understanding.

🏗

Dormitory Tasks

Paper-and-pencil activities designed for zero lab access. Students learn computational thinking without a single computer.
